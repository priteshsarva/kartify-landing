Questions

Good to know.

Do I need my own products?

No. Search Kartify's catalogue — JD, Selloship and more, plus unregistered wholesalers others don't list — and pick what to sell. We handle sourcing and stock; you set your prices and keep the margin. Bringing your own products is optional.

How do my customers pay?

Customers pay by UPI, or send the order to you on WhatsApp to confirm. Our built-in payment gateway verifies payments automatically — the checkout JD and Selloship don't provide.

Can I add sources JD or Selloship don't have?

Yes. Paste any Selloship or JDWebnship store URL and we scrape its products into your catalogue. You also get unregistered wholesalers competitors can't show.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Every store starts on a free name.thekartify.com address, and you can connect any domain you own whenever you're ready — with free SSL.

What does it cost to start?

Nothing. Open your store and take orders on the free plan. Paid plans unlock a custom domain, higher limits and more — only when you need them.
