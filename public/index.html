Questions

Good to know.

Do I need my own products?

No. Pick from Kartify's ready catalogues — we handle sourcing and stock. You choose what to sell, set your prices, and keep the margin. Bringing your own products is optional, not required.

How do my customers pay?

Customers pay by UPI, or send the order to you on WhatsApp to confirm. You can also switch on an automated payment gateway that verifies payments for you — your choice, per store.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. Every store starts on a free name.thekartify.com address, and you can connect any domain you own whenever you're ready.

What does it cost to start?

Nothing. Open your store and take orders on the free plan. Paid plans unlock a custom domain, higher limits and more — only when you need them.
